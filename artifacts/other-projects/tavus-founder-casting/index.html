Tavus limitations found

  • No Nigerian-accented Tavus voice exists on this account: the 40 stock voices carry no accent tags and appear to be General American. Nigerian founders will speak with their actor's own voice.
  • Only three distinct Black stock actors exist on Tavus (Darius, Celine, Destiny). The other two Nigerian founders are written as a Lebanese-Nigerian and an Indian-Nigerian founder, both born in Lagos, which matches real Lagos communities.
  • Tavus stock videos render 16:9; vertical 9:16 versions are made by a centred crop in post-production, with captions and lower thirds added locally (no other voice provider used).

Real companies

All twenty videos are fictional companies. Tavus stock faces are licensed to play fictional characters, and an AI actor saying "I founded Flutterwave" would put words in a real founder's mouth. Real companies are proposed instead as public profiles built only from sourced public facts, marked unclaimed, with no video, no invented numbers and no "verified" badge. That still gives Q real names to search for.
