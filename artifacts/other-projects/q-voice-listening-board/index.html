How should Q sound?

The same ten Q replies spoken by three voice engines. Two things matter here: how natural each one sounds, and how quickly the first sound arrives (measured from Lagos; Railway will be a little faster). Nothing has been switched yet.

Proposed

Move Q's voice from ElevenLabs turbo v2.5 to ElevenLabs v3 Conversational, with the same voice (Sarah).

Why

  • First audio as fast or faster on short replies (about 250–280 ms).
  • The only engine that performs a laugh, chuckle or sigh instead of reading the word aloud.
  • Reads money ranges correctly (“three million”, not “three meters”).
  • Same cost per character.

Trade-offs

  • p95 first audio about 200 ms wider on short text; re-checked on Railway after the switch.
  • Ignores the speed setting, so pace is expressed by pauses only.
  • Laughs and sighs are short. Rows 04–07 are the ones to judge.
